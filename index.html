Dr. Jindong Wang is an Assistant Professor at William & Mary Data Science Department. Previously, he was a Senior Researcher in Microsoft Research Asia from 2019 to 2024. His research interest spans machine learning, large foundation models, and generative AI，aiming to understand and improve AI models to be more robust and trustworthy. He is among World’s Top 2% Highly Cited Scientists. He is action editor of TMLR, associate editor of IEEE TNNLS, area chair for ICML, NeurIPS, ICLR, KDD, AAAI, and ACL, and SPC of IJCAI. He published over 60 papers at top-tier venues (30000+ citations, H-index 64). His research received the ICDM 10-Year Highest Impact Paper Award and best paper awards at several conferences and workshops. His is supported by William & Mary Faculty Research Award, Virginia Commonwealth Cyber Initiative grants, and industrial awards from Amazon, Google, NVIDIA, AMD, and Microsoft. His work was applied into Microsoft health and finance products for accurate and efficient prediction, and also reported by CNN, Forbes, MIT Technology Review, and other international media. He published a book Introduction to Transfer Learning and gave tutorials at top conferences including KDD, CVPR, and IJCAI.
